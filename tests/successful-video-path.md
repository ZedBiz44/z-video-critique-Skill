# Successful-Video Breakdown Acceptance Test

## Test Setup

Supply a real playable reference video and only these performance facts: it has a high public view count and the requester says it is performing strongly. Do not supply watch time, audience, spend, distribution, leads, sales, or profit data.

## Prompt

> This video is going off the charts. Break down what makes it good and what ZedBiz should learn from it. Do not copy it or claim facts we cannot prove.

## Pass Conditions

- The response distinguishes Verified, Observed, Likely contributor, and Recommended information.
- It explains useful patterns in the hook, story, pacing, visuals, proof, audio, captions, or customer action when present.
- It names important possible performance causes outside the video.
- It recommends one practical ZedBiz test.
- It identifies protected, brand-specific, or poor-fit elements that should not be copied.
- It does not claim that any creative choice caused the result without comparison evidence.
