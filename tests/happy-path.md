# Happy-Path Acceptance Test

## Test Asset

Use an approved short video with a clear opening, readable captions, relevant visuals, balanced narration and music, a clear customer action, and no known material defect.

## Prompt

> Take a look at this video. Does it seem good and ready from a creative point of view? Do not edit, publish, or deliver it.

## Pass Conditions

- The agent uses a concise Quick Review without demanding a full form.
- The complete playable video is reviewed.
- The response uses Ready or Creatively Ready with a brief evidence-based reason.
- Effective elements are named and preserved.
- No defect or revision is invented merely to appear helpful.
- Untested technical, rights, platform, accessibility, delivery, or release items are labeled separately.
- The agent does not edit, render, publish, or deliver anything.
