# Video Critique Response Patterns

Use the shortest pattern that fully answers the request. Omit headings that add no value.

## Quick Readiness Review

> **Verdict: Fix.** The message is clear, but the logo covers the caption during the customer action.
>
> **Keep:** Keep the narration, scene footage, caption wording, and timing.
>
> **Change — 00:11-00:14, logo layer:** Move the logo higher and reduce it slightly.
>
> **Done when:** The logo and caption are both readable at normal phone size.

When no material problem exists, use Ready or Creatively Ready and do not invent a change.

## Focused Review

> **Focused finding:** The opening line is strong, but the first supporting visual arrives two seconds after the claim.
>
> **Keep:** Keep the opening narration and presenter performance.
>
> **Change — 00:02-00:04, visual layer:** Bring the demonstration footage in when the claim begins. Keep the words and total timing unchanged.
>
> **Done when:** The viewer sees the proof as the claim is spoken.

## Full Readiness Review

Use only the relevant headings:

- **Verdict**
- **Must fix before delivery**
- **Preserve**
- **Improve if time allows**
- **Open checks**
- **Ship when**

Every must-fix item needs a timestamp or scene, affected layer, exact action, protected elements, and done-when condition. Keep optional polish separate from material corrections.

## Successful-Video Breakdown

- **Bottom line:** Explain in plain language why the video is effective.
- **Performance facts:** List only supplied metrics and confirmed context.
- **What works:** Identify the observed strengths.
- **Likely contributors:** Explain which choices may be helping and why.
- **Other possible causes:** Name important factors outside the video that remain unknown.
- **Reusable patterns:** State principles ZedBiz can adapt.
- **Do not copy:** Identify brand-specific, protected, unsupported, or poor-fit elements.
- **Recommended test:** Give one practical way to test the strongest useful pattern.

Do not turn correlation into proof of cause.

## Comparison

- **Main difference:** State the most important gap first.
- **Keep:** Name what already works in our video.
- **Learn from the reference:** Identify the useful pattern and why it fits our job.
- **Change:** Give timestamped, layer-specific corrections.
- **Done when:** State the observable stopping point.
- **Do not copy:** Name elements that should remain unique to the reference creator or brand.

## Revision Recheck

> **Previous findings:** The caption overlap is resolved. The audio fade remains abrupt at 00:27-00:29.
>
> **Verdict: Fix.** One prior material issue remains.
>
> **Change — 00:27-00:29, music layer:** Extend the fade through the final spoken word. Keep narration, visuals, captions, and total duration unchanged.
>
> **Done when:** The music ends smoothly after the narration without a sudden cut.

Close fixed findings before discussing anything new. Do not reopen settled preferences.

## Unable to Complete

> **Review not completed.** The supplied item is a thumbnail or inaccessible path, so pacing, motion, narration, audio, transitions, and final playback cannot be judged. Please provide the complete playable video file or an accessible shared file.

State any limited observation separately. Never present a still-image check as a complete video critique.
