# Channel and Platform Notes

Use this reference only when the requester names a channel, delivery setting, or verified platform requirement.

## Evidence Rule

Treat supplied current platform documentation, approved client specifications, and direct inspection as evidence. Treat remembered or general platform rules as unverified until checked. Platform requirements change.

## Creative Checks

Review whether the video's orientation, framing, caption placement, opening speed, text size, subject crop, and customer action suit the stated viewing conditions. Distinguish a visible creative problem from an unverified technical requirement.

## Technical Boundary

Do not certify resolution, frame rate, codec, file size, bitrate, loudness, duration limit, safe areas, link behavior, upload success, accessibility, rights, or policy compliance unless the exact requirement and final file were checked.

If a current requirement matters to the verdict and no verified source is available, label it as an open final check for production or the responsible publisher. Do not fail otherwise effective creative because of an invented platform rule.

## Common Viewing Contexts

- **Short vertical social video:** Check immediate clarity, subject crop, caption readability, and whether important text stays visible in the supplied platform preview.
- **Paid advertisement:** Check message-offer fit, proof, brand recognition, caption clarity, and the requested customer action. Separate creative readiness from advertising approval and performance claims.
- **Website or landing-page video:** Check page-context fit, opening clarity without sound when relevant, caption availability when supplied, and whether the video supports the page's next step.
- **Training or demonstration:** Check instructional order, readable demonstrations, enough time to follow steps, and accurate narration-to-visual alignment.
- **Presenter or spokesperson video:** Check believable performance, eye line, body movement, identity consistency, narration continuity, and lip-sync.

Do not force short-form advertising conventions onto a training, story, demonstration, or long-form educational video.
