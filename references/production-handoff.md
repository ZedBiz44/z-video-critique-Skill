# Z Video Production and Critique Handoff

Use this agreement when `z-video-production` sends a new or meaningfully edited Structured or Managed video for independent review.

## Responsibility Split

- **Production:** brief, production route, source media, editing, corrections, rendering, technical checks, saving, and delivery.
- **Critique:** independent creative verdict, material findings, protected elements, and observable done-when conditions.
- **Authorized approver:** material creative-direction changes, added spending, client delivery, and publication.

A creative verdict is not release authority.

## Shared Status Terms

- **Working Draft:** Production is still building the first complete playable version.
- **Review Draft:** The complete playable video is ready for critique.
- **proposed Approved Master:** Creative critique passed, but the authorized approver has not accepted release.
- **Published/Delivered:** The authorized external action occurred.

Critique returns only **Ready**, **Creatively Ready**, **Fix**, or **Rebuild** for readiness reviews.

## Required Review Packet

Provide the actual complete playable Review Draft and the most useful available context:

- video version and draft status;
- intended use, audience, and desired response;
- brief, script, or approved dry narration master when relevant;
- target length, orientation, and platform when known;
- approved brand assets and references;
- exact words, offer, price, URL, and customer action that must remain correct;
- elements that must remain unchanged; and
- previous critique findings for a revision.

The approved dry narration master remains the timing and performance source when narration is part of the project. Keep audio as a separate production layer; critique judges the audible result without silently replacing the approved master.

Do not represent a path, render log, thumbnail, description, or selected still frames as the complete Review Draft.

## Separate Review Requirement

For Structured or Managed work, perform critique in a separate fresh session or with a different authorized reviewing agent. Production must not approve its own work by merely rereading the same instructions inside the production run.

Until an automatic cross-session handoff has been built and verified, production prepares the review packet and gives the requester the exact separate critique request. Do not claim an automatic transfer occurred.

## Apply a Fix Without Scope Drift

Production changes only the affected layers named in the critique. Preserve the approved script, narration, timing, identity, offer, customer action, brand treatment, provider, duration, aspect ratio, and format unless the authorized approver changes them.

Production renders a new complete playable version. Critique begins the targeted recheck with the prior material findings and closes fixed items before raising any material new issue.

## Final Export

After creative review passes, production renders and technically validates the exact delivery export. Critique rechecks the export when rendering, compression, cropping, captions, timing, or audio mixing could have affected an earlier material finding.

Keep Review Drafts, corrected versions, the proposed Approved Master, final export, and editable project distinct. Do not overwrite the only original or approved master.

## If Critique Is Unavailable

Production may complete its basic full-timeline review and must record exactly:

**Basic video review completed; ZedBiz video critique companion unavailable.**

That fallback does not close a required critique gate for work that explicitly needs full critique or is costly, high-risk, client-facing, or publication-ready.
